First step: any bottom-row square may be tried (wrong entrance also fails). After that, only squares adjacent to the current position can be tried. A correct step keeps the current member at the front; a wrong step shows ✕ for 3 seconds, clears progress, and moves that member to the back of the queue. All 10 routes have the same length (12 squares) for equal difficulty.
